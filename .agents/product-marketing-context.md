# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-04-19*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Dealvinci is a credit-powered price intelligence platform for teams that need to catch price drops fast.
**What it does:** Dealvinci turns product URLs into active trackers, monitors prices on recurring intervals, and sends actionable alerts to channels operators already watch. Users can set tracking mode, target prices, and check frequency from one dashboard.
**Product category:** Price tracking software; ecommerce price intelligence; deal monitoring.
**Product type:** SaaS.
**Business model:** Tiered subscription with monthly credits. Credits are spent on monitoring activity and plan tiers define active tracker limits, minimum check intervals, and alert channel access.
- Starter: $0/month, 1,500 credits, up to 10 active trackers, 60-minute minimum interval, email + in-app notifications.
- Growth: $29/month, 12,000 credits, up to 150 active trackers, 15-minute minimum interval, Telegram alerts + daily summaries.
- Scale: $99/month, 50,000 credits, up to 1,000 active trackers, 5-minute minimum interval, Telegram + webhook alerts, higher throughput/support SLA.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Solo operators, resellers, and small-to-mid ecommerce/sourcing teams that depend on timing and margin.
**Decision-makers:** Founder, ecommerce operations lead, sourcing lead, performance-oriented power user.
**Primary use case:** Track large sets of product URLs and get alerted immediately when a target price is reached.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Turn a product URL into a live tracker quickly.
- Replace manual repeated tab-checking with automated monitoring.
- Get high-signal price alerts where the team already works (especially Telegram).
**Use cases:**
- Deal-hunting and sourcing across multiple stores.
- Monitoring products for resale/arbitrage opportunities.
- Running ongoing watchlists for planned purchases.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Teams need continuous price monitoring, but manual workflows are repetitive and easy to miss.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Manual spreadsheets/bookmarks require constant attention.
- Store-native alerts are inconsistent and fragmented.
- Generic monitoring tools do not provide an operator-focused workflow.
**What it costs them:** Missed deals, slower decisions, lower margin capture, and wasted hours.
**Emotional tension:** FOMO, uncertainty, and distrust that alerts will arrive on time.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Tools like Keepa, CamelCamelCamel, Visualping, and Distill - useful, but often fragmented or less aligned to multi-store operator workflows.
**Secondary:** Store-native wishlist/price alert systems - convenient but isolated per retailer and hard to manage at scale.
**Indirect:** Manual checking with tabs, reminders, and spreadsheets - flexible but unreliable and time intensive.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Credit-powered usage model tied to real monitoring demand.
- Clear tier packaging for volume, speed, and alert-channel needs.
- URL-to-tracker workflow designed for operators, not technical scraping users.
- Telegram-first alerting for immediate action.
**How we do it differently:** Dealvinci packages scraping/monitoring capability into a focused operational product with tracker controls, thresholds, and dependable alert routing.
**Why that's better:** Users move from data collection to fast action without building custom scripts or checking pages manually.
**Why customers choose us:** It is practical, fast to activate, and tuned for margin-driven teams.
