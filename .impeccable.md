## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are price-tracking operators who want to add products quickly, monitor status, and receive Telegram notifications without friction.
- They need high scanability for many tracked items, quick state changes (pause/resume/delete), and confidence that alerts are connected and working.
- Primary job to be done: go from URL to active tracker with clear status and dependable notification flow.

### Brand Personality
- Clean, sleek, modern, and product-confident.
- Operational and trustworthy, not playful or decorative.
- Emotional goals: calm control, speed, and confidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
- References favor a refined neutral base with crisp contrast, subtle elevation, and precise spacing.
- Avoid dull grayscale monotony and avoid generic dashboard sameness.
- Use restrained accent color with purpose (status, action hierarchy, progress), not random rainbow accents.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize operational clarity over decoration: every visual cue should help scanning or decision-making.
2. Keep one visual focal action per section: avoid competing primaries.
3. Use purposeful color semantics: neutrals for structure, accent for action/state, semantic tones for feedback.
4. Preserve progressive disclosure in tracker creation: URL fetch first, preferences second.
5. Maintain smooth, subtle motion that reinforces context changes, never distracts from task flow.
