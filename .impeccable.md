## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are solo shoppers tracking personal purchases and deal targets.
- They need fast, low-friction flows to add links, set alert/monitor mode, and trust status at a glance.
- Primary job to be done: go from product URL to reliable tracking + notification with minimal cognitive load.

### Brand Personality
- Calm, controlled, and trustworthy.
- Operational confidence over novelty.
- Emotional goals: calm control, speed, and reliability.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Refined neutral base, high scanability, subtle elevation, and precise spacing.
- Borderless-by-default visual language: structure should come from spacing, tone, and hierarchy, not outlined boxes.
- Use restrained accent color only for status/action meaning; avoid decorative gradients, noisy chrome, and playful gimmicks.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize shopper clarity over decoration: every cue must improve scanning or decisions.
2. Keep one focal action per section; avoid competing primaries and crowded control rows.
3. Maintain borderless hierarchy: use spacing, contrast, and surface tone instead of visible borders.
4. Preserve progressive disclosure in tracker setup: URL/product confirmation first, rules second.
5. Keep motion subtle and confidence-building, with reduced-motion-safe behavior and accessible contrast.
