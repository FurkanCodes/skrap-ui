## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are deal hunters, sourcing teams, and operators who need reliable product-price monitoring.
- They work in a dashboard context where credits, tracker limits, Telegram delivery, and item status must be understood quickly.
- Primary job to be done: turn product URLs into live trackers, choose alert or price-drop mode, manage limits, and trust notification delivery with minimal cognitive load.

### Brand Personality
- Calm, controlled, and trustworthy.
- Practical, precise, and quietly intelligent.
- Emotional goals: calm control, operational confidence, and reliable speed.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Refined neutral base with blue/cyan primary accents, high scanability, subtle elevation, and precise spacing.
- Workspace surfaces should feel dense, quiet, and operational: compact controls, clear status, low ornament.
- Marketing pages may be warmer and more illustrative, but must still keep product value concrete and avoid generic SaaS spectacle.
- Support both light and dark themes using existing OKLCH tokens and Plus Jakarta Sans in app chrome; avoid ad hoc palettes.
- Use restrained accent color for status, action, and selection meaning; avoid decorative gradients, noisy chrome, and playful gimmicks.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize operator clarity over decoration: every cue must improve scanning, trust, or decision speed.
2. Keep one focal action per section; avoid competing primaries and crowded control rows.
3. Use structure sparingly: prefer spacing, contrast, and surface tone; reserve borders for forms, modals, lists, and error states.
4. Preserve progressive disclosure in tracker setup: URLs first, product confirmation second, alert rules third.
5. Make plan limits, credits, Telegram readiness, loading, success, and error states explicit.
6. Keep motion subtle and confidence-building, with reduced-motion-safe behavior and accessible contrast.
